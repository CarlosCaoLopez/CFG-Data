The Challenge

Luck, or
information?

  • Hundreds of markets close every day — sports, politics, crypto, pop culture
  • Every trade is permanently public on-chain: wallet, amount, timing, outcome — all visible to anyone
  • The signal is subtle: not just "did they win?" — when they bet, how much, how old the wallet, how focused their portfolio
  • You can't review this manually. You need a system that watches everything, 24/7, and decides automatically

Layer A · L1 MarketCloseListener

Two Eyes on Every Market

Eye #1 — Blockchain WebSocket
  • Listens to raw OrderFilled events emitted by the CTFExchange contract on Polygon RPC
  • Polymarket's own WebSocket is anonymous — the blockchain isn't. This is how we get wallet addresses.
  • Discovers a market the instant it has on-chain activity — zero lag, continuous
Eye #2 — Gamma API (every 15 min)
  • Safety net for quiet markets — low activity means few on-chain events the WebSocket would catch
  • Sweeps all open markets above volume threshold: zero-gap coverage
  • Together: every relevant market is tracked, regardless of activity level
Every 60s →

Close detection loop polls each tracked market. Market closed? Collect everything.

On Market Close

Market closed. Go.

1 Fetch every single trade ever placed on that market via the Polymarket Data API
2 For each wallet: look up its age on the Polygon blockchain via Etherscan V2 (chainid=137) — not just Polymarket activity. A wallet created 3 days ago for one big bet is a red flag that needs to be measured.
3 Normalize everything into a single validated MarketDataBatch Pydantic contract: trades, wallet ages, market metadata
4 Push to Redis Streams — consumer groups with explicit ACK, survives restarts, at-least-once guaranteed

Task T2 · Celery · SQL

Five Numbers That Tell a Story

VariableWhat it measuresFormulaThreshold
Δt Hours between last trade and market close — the closer to the wire, the more suspicious MIN(end_date − timestamp) per wallet ≤ P1%
Age Wallet age on Polygon in days — burner wallets are days old, established traders are years old MAX(1, end_date − first_seen_at) ≤ P1%
Win.Vol Net USDC placed on the correct outcome, weighted by trade direction (buy/sell) and result Σ size × prize × side × outcome sign ≥ P99%
α Wallet's share of total market volume — did they move the price with their position? Σ size_usdc / market.volume_usdc ≥ P99%
H Portfolio entropy across categories — a focused, specialist wallet is more suspicious −Σ P·ln(P) over all categories ⚑ disabled
Layer D · R + Plumber

The Judge

  • Receives only market_id via HTTP — reads the computed variables directly from the database
  • Empirical percentile thresholds — no parametric distribution assumptions. Polymarket data is fat-tailed, non-normal, and weird.
  • A lucky wallet hits one or two signals. An insider hits all four simultaneously.
Criterion — all 4 required simultaneously
Δt ≤ P 1% Traded near the wire
Age ≤ P 1% Very young wallet
Win.Vol ≥ P 99% Extreme winning bet
α ≥ P 99% Moved the market
Verdict — when all 4 are met 4 critical regions → INSIDER

The Alert Fires

Motor D POST /insider-detection → FastAPI receives the verdict: which wallets, which categories
FastAPI E Sets wallet.insider = true in PostgreSQL, stores categories_insider and market IDs
FastAPI E Deletes the temporary variables table for that market — job done, data cleaned up, inline
Task T3 Publishes ADD_WALLET command to Redis stream insider_commands — L2 picks it up immediately
L2 Watcher Polls the wallet's trades every 30 seconds, filters by insider categories, stores in insider_trades permanently
Result The wallet is under continuous surveillance. Every future suspicious trade is recorded in real-time.
